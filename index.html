01 / The problem

A simple transport job can become a chain of calls, slips, waiting, and middlemen.

In many transport workflows, requirements move between people instead of moving through one connected system. Information gets repeated, options are hard to compare, and coordination becomes more complicated than the shipment itself.

Digi_TransX started from direct exposure to the transport industry through family background and one question: can software become the bridge between the people already doing this work?

01

Fragmented communication

Important details often travel through separate phone calls and conversations.

02

Too many handoffs

Each additional intermediary can add delay, confusion, and duplicated coordination.

03

Harder comparison

When offers are scattered, choosing the right transporter becomes less transparent.

04

Disconnected trip flow

Booking, payment, communication, and completion can feel like separate processes.

02 / The approach

One digital bridge. Different people. One connected process.

Digi_TransX is designed around a marketplace workflow where transport needs are structured, relevant transporters can respond, and the selected trip continues through a connected digital journey.

REQ

Structured requirements

Service seekers describe the shipment in a consistent format instead of rebuilding the same information across calls.

BID

Transporter bidding

Eligible transporters can respond to demand through a clearer bid-based marketplace flow.

PAY

Test checkout logic

Internal testing uses dummy checkout as part of the trip workflow; no live payment gateway is connected.

TRIP

Trip coordination

Selection, communication, trip status, and completion belong to the same project workflow.

03 / How it is designed to work

From transport requirement to completed trip.

The platform will continue evolving during development, but this is the core journey Digi_TransX is being built around.

01

Create a transport requirement

The service seeker enters shipment, route, vehicle, capacity, and other required details.

02

Relevant transporters respond

The requirement becomes available to matching transporters who can submit their offers.

03

Compare and select

The seeker reviews participating transporters and chooses the offer that fits the requirement.

04

Test checkout and trip activation

The selected trip proceeds through dummy checkout during internal testing; no real payment is processed.

05

Coordinate the trip

Transporter and client stay connected through the platform for trip-related communication and status.

06

Confirm completion

The workflow closes with trip completion and client confirmation instead of leaving the final step outside the system.

For service seekers

Describe the need. Compare responses. Choose with more clarity.

  • Structured order creation
  • Transporter offers in one place
  • Trip communication
  • Connected completion flow

For transporters

See relevant demand and respond through a digital marketplace.

  • Vehicle-based matching direction
  • Bid on suitable requirements
  • Manage selected trips
  • Keep coordination connected

05 / Long-term direction

Not another layer in transport. A bridge between the layers already there.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary friction while respecting how transport actually works. Digi_TransX is being built from the ground up around practical logistics problems rather than around technology for technology's sake.
