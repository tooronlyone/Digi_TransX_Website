Participant Roles & Commercial Driver Boundaries

Every account acts within strict capability guardrails. Dedicated driver accountability prevents off-platform undercutting, while owner-operator checks verify real licensing before self-driving.

Everyday & Business Seekers

Demand Side

Individuals moving household or personal shipments alongside commercial teams shipping repeatable one-time cargo—handled inside one consistent bidding and delivery verification model.

Permitted Actions

  • Post structured shipment specifications
  • Inspect offers backed by concrete trucks & fleet ratings
  • Hold funds in escrow flow, verify cargo, share OTP, or dispute

System Guardrail

  • Reviews open only after genuine OTP closure
  • Unilateral post-dispatch cancellations follow platform fairness rules

Fleet Transporters

Fleet Supply

Truck owners managing one or multiple registered vehicles from a central account—viewing eligible orders filtered to truck fit and operating area, then bidding directly with zero auto-dispatch lock-in.

Permitted Actions

  • Register multiple trucks with payload & service bounds
  • Bid openly with a selected compatible vehicle
  • Assign trips to permanent employees or per-trip dispatches

System Guardrail

  • Sees rule-matched eligible loads rather than noise
  • Bidding commits fleet availability and escrow readiness

Owner-Operators (Self-Drive Mode)

Dual Verified

Independent fleet owners who drive their own vehicles. Choosing “Drive Myself” unlocks only when active fleet truck ownership and verified commercial driving credentials both pass check.

Permitted Actions

  • Bid as transporter and take the wheel cleanly via self-drive
  • Receive trip execution controls without hiring a middle driver
  • Submit trip fuel and operational updates under one roof

Credential Gate

  • Unverified fleets cannot self-assign without licensed driver approval
  • Remains bound to single active trip execution safeguards

Commercial Drivers

Strict Guardrails

Dedicated drivers onboarded by unique system ID under either per-trip dispatch or permanent fleet contracts—giving fleet owners clear trip visibility while protecting drivers from ambiguous instructions.

Permitted Actions

  • Review & accept or decline dispatched fleet assignments
  • Execute one active accepted trip at a time with conflict lock
  • Coordinate in trip chat, log trip fuel receipts & incident reports, send active-trip check-ins

Explicitly Blocked

  • Cannot bypass fleet owners to post shipment orders directly
  • Cannot place competitive bids on the marketplace independently
  • Cannot double-accept overlapping active runs

How Fairness & Closure Work Conceptually

Digi_TransX builds structural checkpoints into every handoff—keeping incentives balanced across seekers, transporters, and drivers without relying on blind trust or revealing private internal tuning.

Rule-Assisted Choice, Never Forced Auto-Dispatch

Compatibility logic filters orders against truck specs, readiness, and service reach so unsuitable vehicles never waste bids—while leaving the final carrier choice strictly between the seeker and bidding fleets.

Match rules serve signal quality; human selection stays intact.

Inspect-First Delivery OTP & Staged Settlement

Truck arrival is never treated as blind automatic payout. Seekers inspect unloaded cargo first and hand over a one-time completion code only when satisfied, triggering staged internal settlement cleanly.

Physical condition governs handover closure at the unloading bay.

Frozen Dispute Review & Audited Trip Logs

When cargo expectations clash, disputes pause remaining release right away and move evidence, contextual messages, and incident or fuel records directly to internal admin arbitration.

Internal formulas, reserves, and escalation thresholds stay guarded.

Honest Pre-Launch Engineering Status

Digi_TransX is built cleanly in private development—not publicly launched. Rather than overclaiming production readiness, here is the exact line between working code, active security work, and intentionally offline infrastructure.

Exercised across internal one-time test runs.

  • Role boundaries for everyday users, business seekers, fleets, owner-operators, drivers, and platform admin
  • Multi-truck fleet configuration & rule-based truck compatibility filtering

Active Pre-Pilot Hardening

Under active cleanup prior to any closed rollout.

  • Authentication, session lifecycle, and multi-device access controls
  • Sensitive action and secondary security PIN hardening
  • Backend-to-frontend endpoint contract alignment and flow cleanup
  • Role verification consistency across internal admin review queues

Not Live / Intentionally Offline

Absent right now—stated candidly with zero spin.

  • No public user signups, commercial onboarding, or live marketplace traffic
  • Checkout uses internal dummy test-card workflows only—no live payment gateway or real bank withdrawals
  • Dedicated external vehicle GPS tracker hardware integration remains offline and planned (internal app builds use trip-scoped smartphone check-ins only during accepted driver runs)
